Introducing myself

I received my Bachelor degree in Audiovisual Telecommunication Systems Engineering in 2011. In 2014, I obtained a M.S. degree in Artificial Intelligence from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia and a partial M.S. degree in Computer Vision from the Autonomous University of Barcelona, Spain. As a research assistant, I have worked for several projects devoted to physical rehabilitation and active aging. I have worked for the last 3 years at Noldus Information Techonology, the Netherlands, as a Marie Curie Early Stage Researcher on automatic methods for automatic analysis of parent-child interactions, and now I am continuing my PhD at University of Barcelona, Spain, focusing on automatic gaze estimation with remote cameras.
