Introducing myself

I received my Bachelor degree in Audiovisual Telecommunication Systems Engineering in 2011. In 2014, I obtained a M.S. degree in Artificial Intelligence from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia and a partial M.S. degree in Computer Vision from the Autonomous University of Barcelona, Spain. In 2024, I obtained my PhD in Engineering and Applied Sciences, entitled 'Gaze Estimation with Spatiotemporal and Multimodal Deep Learning' from Universitat de Barcelona, Spain. During the past decade, I have worked on several projects devoted to physical rehabilitation, active aging, and human behavior understanding and synthesis. Among others, I worked for 3 years at Noldus Information Techonology, the Netherlands, as a Marie Curie Early Stage Researcher, and I interned twice at the Eye Tracking Research team at Reality Labs, Meta (US). I am currently a deep learning and computer vision consultant and freelancer.
